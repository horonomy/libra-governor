# HORO-1154 — Interview / follow-up questions

Real conversation starters, not a yes/no survey (per the ticket's
explicit requirement). Use these to open each theme, then follow the
participant's actual answer wherever it leads — the goal is genuine
qualitative signal, not a completed checklist.

## Perceived friction / admission latency

- "Walk me through what it actually felt like the first few times
  Libra ran a preflight or interrupted you for a replan — what were you
  thinking in that moment?"
- "Was there ever a point where you found yourself working around it,
  ignoring it, or wishing it would just get out of the way? What was
  happening right before that?"
- "If you compare a task with Libra running to the same kind of task
  without it — what's actually different in how the session felt, not
  just in the numbers?"

## Replan usefulness / annoyance

- "Tell me about a specific replan you remember — what triggered it, and
  did the interruption feel justified in hindsight?"
- "Has a replan ever surprised you — either 'why didn't it replan
  sooner' or 'why did it replan for that'?"

## Second-use / repeat behavior

- "Did you keep reaching for Libra as the two weeks went on, or did your
  usage change — more, less, or about the same? What changed it?"
- "Was there ever a session where you deliberately avoided using it? Why
  that session specifically?"
- "If I hadn't asked you to do this evaluation, do you think you'd still
  be using it right now? Be honest — 'no' is a completely fine answer
  and exactly what I need to hear."

## Would route more work through it

- "Thinking about the kinds of tasks you didn't run through Libra during
  this evaluation — what kept you from using it there? Would you,
  going forward?"
- "What would have to be true about Libra for you to trust it with a
  bigger, riskier task than the ones you tried it on?"

## Team / shared-policy / Codex demand

- "Does your team currently have any shared convention, tooling, or
  process for controlling agent cost/time/scope? What is it, if
  anything?"
- "If your teammates saw what you saw using Libra, what do you think
  their reaction would be — genuinely curious, indifferent, or
  skeptical? Why?"
- "Do you use Codex (or another agent tool) enough that not having
  Libra there matters to you? What would you actually want it to do
  there?"

## Paid / design-partner interest

- "If Libra graduated from this free developer preview to something you
  had to pay for, what would that decision look like for you — who
  would need to be convinced, and what would they need to see?"
- "Is there a version of 'paying for this' that makes sense to you —
  a subscription, a team license, something else? What wouldn't make
  sense?"
- "Would you be interested in a deeper design-partner conversation about
  where this goes next — not a sales pitch, an actual say in the
  roadmap?"

## Closing

- "What's the one thing about this you'd change first, if you could
  change exactly one thing?"
- "Anything I should have asked and didn't?"

## Recording guidance

Capture answers close to verbatim (not paraphrased into a forced
yes/no) — the analysis template (`analysis-report-template.md`)
explicitly asks for willingness-to-pay and other signals verbatim, not
summarized into a fabricated binary.

Unsupported: Windows.

The header above ("before pushing a `v*` tag") and row 12 are kept as written.
The documented D5 exception does not change them; it records why they cannot be
met inside the frozen SHA. The first-publication release-notes gate that D5 left open is closed in engineering by PR #259: `release.yml` now publishes `docs/release-notes/<tag>.md` as the body of the first public Release, refuses to publish when it is missing, empty or lacks a mandatory disclosure, and does not generate notes. A manual edit after creation remains NOT accepted as a first-publication guarantee. The real tag path is confirmed only by the post-publication body comparison.
